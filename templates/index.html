HOW TO PLAY
1
Find an opponent

Press FIND OPPONENT and wait for another connected player.

2
Countdown

Both players see the same 3 → 2 → 1 duel countdown.

3
Reveal

The server deals one card to each player from a shuffled 52-card deck.

4
Compare

A, K, Q, J and numbered cards are compared by rank value. Same rank = draw.

5
Play again

The result screen lets both players return and start another match.
